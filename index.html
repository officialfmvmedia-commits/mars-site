01 — LIGHT FROM ANOTHER WORLD

Sunlight hit the surface of Mars. It bounced up, crossed space, and landed on a camera in orbit.

On August 15, 2026, NASA's Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter pointed its HiRISE camera at the layered terrain near 6.5°S and captured it in color. That image is public. Anyone on Earth can download it, byte for byte.

$MARS is the first coin whose mint key is seeded from that light. Not a mascot. Not a metaphor. A real photograph of another planet, hashed and folded into the keypair that creates the token.

03 — VERIFY IT YOURSELF

Commit, then reveal

Before launch: we publish the hash of our private entropy. That locks it in; it can't be swapped later.

Mint: the Mars hash + entropy derive the mint key. Mint authority is renounced.

After: we reveal the entropy. Anyone can rerun HKDF and land on the exact contract address.

Why it's safe: without the 256 secret bits, the public Mars hash reveals nothing about the key. Guessing would take 2256 tries.
